## 1. INSTITUTIONAL THREATS - Attacking the State

### 1.1 Violating Democratic Norms & Undermining Rule of Law
- **Examples:** Threatening to withhold federal funds based on elections, ignoring court orders
- **Pattern:** Direct attacks on democratic processes and legal institutions
- **Count:** ~800+ actions

### 1.2 Hollowing State / Weakening Federal Institutions
- **Examples:** Mass firings of agency leadership, defunding government programs
- **Pattern:** Dismantling institutional capacity and expertise
- **Count:** ~300+ actions

### 1.3 Suppressing Dissent / Weaponizing State Against 'Enemies'
- **Examples:** Targeting journalists, charging protesters, investigating critics
- **Pattern:** Using state power to silence opposition
- **Count:** ~500+ actions

## 2. INFORMATION THREATS - Controlling the Narrative

### 2.1 Controlling Information / Spreading Misinformation and Propaganda
- **Examples:** Editing interviews, restricting press access, spreading false claims
- **Pattern:** Manipulating public understanding of events
- **Count:** ~400+ actions

### 2.2 Control of Science & Health to Align with State Ideology
- **Examples:** CDC censorship, climate denial, RFK Jr health policies
- **Pattern:** Replacing evidence with ideology
- **Count:** ~250+ actions

## 3. SOCIETAL THREATS - Attacking Individuals

### 3.1 Weakening Civil Rights
- **Examples:** Restricting gender-affirming care, voter purges, medical debt policies
- **Pattern:** Eroding legal protections for vulnerable groups
- **Count:** ~450+ actions

### 3.2 Anti-immigrant or Militarized Nationalism
- **Examples:** ICE raids, detention camps, military border operations
- **Pattern:** Dehumanizing immigrants and militarizing enforcement
- **Count:** ~600+ actions

### 3.3 Corruption & Enrichment
- **Examples:** Family crypto profits, business conflicts of interest
- **Pattern:** Personal financial gain from public office
- **Count:** ~200+ actions

## 4. CULTURAL THREATS - Attacking Culture

### 4.1 Attacking Universities, Schools, Museums, Culture
- **Examples:** Firing arts boards, closing Radio Free Asia, "woke" targeting
- **Pattern:** Controlling cultural institutions and education
- **Count:** ~150+ actions

### 4.2 Aggressive Foreign Policy & Global Destabilization
- **Examples:** Unauthorized military strikes, nuclear testing threats, Venezuela threats
- **Pattern:** Reckless militarism without oversight
- **Count:** ~350+ actions

## Key Insights from Grouping

### Overlapping Threats
Many actions fall into multiple categories simultaneously, revealing coordinated attacks:
- **ICE operations** = Civil Rights + Anti-Immigrant + Suppressing Dissent
- **Firing investigators** = Weakening Institutions + Democratic Norms + Suppressing Dissent
- **Press restrictions** = Controlling Information + Democratic Norms + Suppressing Dissent

### Escalation Patterns
1. **Early:** Rhetoric and norm violations
2. **Middle:** Institutional capture and policy changes
3. **Late:** Enforcement through state power and violence

### Target Hierarchy
```
Primary Targets → Democracy & Rule of Law
     ↓
Secondary → Federal Institutions & Expertise
     ↓
Tertiary → Opposition & Dissent
     ↓
Vulnerable → Immigrants, Minorities, Rights-holders
```

## How to Use This Grouping

1. **Identify Patterns:** Multiple actions in one category reveal systematic campaigns
2. **Track Escalation:** Watch for movement from rhetoric to enforcement
3. **Connect Actions:** Related categories show coordinated authoritarian strategies
4. **Predict Next Steps:** Category gaps may indicate future target areas
